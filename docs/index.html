Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our co-managed IT model.

Do you replace our in-house IT department?

Absolutely not. We specialize in co-managed IT. Our goal is to empower your existing IT team, not replace them. We act as an expert extension of your department, handling complex cloud architecture, escalations, and operational runbooks so your team can focus on day-to-day organizational challenges.

Where are you located, and do you support businesses outside your area?

We have been headquartered in the Miami area since February 2014. While we have deep local roots, our cloud advisory and specialized support services are designed for remote delivery, allowing us to seamlessly partner with SMBs nationwide.

What does "results-first cloud advisory" mean for an SMB?

It means we avoid technology for technology's sake. We align cloud solutions directly with your business goals, implementing security, cost optimizations, and operational improvements with minimal disruption to your current workflow.

How does the handoff process work after you build a solution?

We build alongside your team. Once a project or migration is complete, we leave behind clear documentation and repeatable tooling to ensure your internal IT staff can easily manage and maintain the environment.

How secure is your member portal and remote support?

Security is built into our foundation. We utilize enterprise-grade security patterns, including Microsoft Entra ID verification, to ensure that all member sign-ins, remote advisory sessions, and data interactions are strictly protected.

What is the Siucloud AI chatbot, and how do I access it?

The Siucloud AI is a proprietary chatbot widget designed to provide instant, intelligent cloud consultation and troubleshooting. Active members can access it 24/7 by securely signing into our portal using their Entra ID credentials with our Tenant Helpdesk.miami for access to our Resources and consultant on private chat communication.
